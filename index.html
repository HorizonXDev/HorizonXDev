About me

Founder, full-stack developer and extension maker.

I'm Horizon, a developer and founder from North India. I started Cloth Savvy — an AI-powered fashion app that studies your body type, style and the weather to put together outfits, then renders them on your own photo with virtual try-on, so you can see the look before you buy it.

Before that I spent a lot of time in the MIT App Inventor community making extensions. My TicTacToe extension has around 26k views and downloads on GetAix, and on the MIT App Inventor community have reached about 89k views. More recently I made a Chess extension with a built-in AI opponent.

The full Cloth Savvy has been built by me — the Flutter app, App Architecture, backend, infrastructure and the AI pipeline behind the try-on — and I still put out extensions and tools for App Inventor developers when I get the time.
